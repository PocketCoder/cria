Turn 3 · dark mode

Ledger after dark

Not an inversion. The paper/pane relationship is preserved — the content pane still sits lighter than the canvas — but every fill drops into a warm-neutral charcoal, the accent lifts to stay legible, and the one ink-filled element in light mode (buttons, selected rows) flips to a light fill with dark text.

Three rules that change

  • Ink fill inverts. Every ink-filled element — primary buttons, the FAB, the selected palette row — becomes oklch(92% .004 85) with dark text.
  • Selected nav rows don't. A full light fill in a sidebar is too loud; they get oklch(30% .012 265) and weight 500 instead.
  • Shadows stop working. Overlay panels swap their drop shadow for a 1px oklch(34% .008 265) border plus a deeper, wider scrim.

The priority ramp lifts to stay visible on charcoal: high 80% .12 85, urgent 72% .16 45, critical 66% .19 25.
